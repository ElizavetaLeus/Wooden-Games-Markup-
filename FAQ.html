FAQ

Shipping
When do you roast and ship?
An order placed through the Red Rooster website gets roasted and shipped to-order,
typically on the following business day. Orders shipped via USPS will leave our premises
first thing the following morning.

For example, if you place your order at 9am EST on Friday, your order will roast on
Monday and ship on Tuesday morning. If you place your order Monday morning at 9am,
it will roast on Tuesday and ship on Wednesday.

Weekends, sales and holidays will often delay shipments by a day or more.
